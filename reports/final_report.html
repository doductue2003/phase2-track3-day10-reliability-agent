Cache Enabled vs. Cache Disabled Analysis

Impact of Semantic Caching

Adding the 3-gram cosine similarity cache dramatically reduced load on backend LLM providers. Without caching, the system repeatedly queried the flaky primary provider, causing 22 circuit open events. With caching enabled, identical or semantically similar prompts were answered instantly from Redis, dropping provider traffic by over 73%, lowering costs by 74.5%, and decreasing circuit breaks to just 8.

Resilient Gateway Architecture Design
Vulnerability Analysis & Recommended mitigations

Remaining Weakness

Currently, **Circuit Breaker states** are stored strictly in-memory (local to each Node instance). If we scale our gateway horizontally to 10 instances, their breaker counters are completely isolated. This can lead to a **Retry Storm**, where all 10 instances independently trigger probe requests against a crashing provider, compounding the provider's failure.

Proposed Resolution

We recommend moving the circuit breaker counters and state variables (`failure_count`, `state`, `opened_at`) into **Redis**. By using atomic operations like `INCR` and setting a key TTL, all gateway instances will share a unified circuit breaker state, completely neutralizing retry storms.
